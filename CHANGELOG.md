### Added

- Add the Fragility and Velocity potion variants

### Changed

- Update Primal Pearl (and its variants) textures (smaller and animated)

## [21.1-0.1.1]

### Changed

- The Unstoppable Force usage in dimensions other than Overworld is now configurable

- Don't tag pot when breaking potted Myosotis
- Don't consume Ender Myosotis when used in the wrong dimension

### Added

- Port all features to NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.1
  - Myosotis and Ender Myosotis
  - Primal Pearl and its variants (Plant, Animal, Aquatic, Death)
  - The Unstoppable Force and the Heart of Glass
  - JEI descriptions

- Change LICENSE to `Apache License 2.0`
